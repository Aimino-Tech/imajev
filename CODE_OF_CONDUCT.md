# Code of Conduct

We pledge to maintain an open, welcoming and professional community.

Be respectful, constructive and evidence-focused. Harassment, sexualized conduct, trolling, derogatory comments, doxxing and other inappropriate professional conduct are not accepted.

Maintainers may remove contributions or interactions that violate these standards. Reports can be made privately to repository maintainers and will be handled with appropriate confidentiality.

Adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html
